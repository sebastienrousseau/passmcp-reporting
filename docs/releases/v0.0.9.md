## Highlights ⭐️

* **Processor runtime and gRPC updates**: the `agentgateway-extmcp` container builds on `golang:1.27.3-bookworm` pinned by digest, requires `passmcp-reporting` `v0.0.8` (ADR 0002), and upgrades `google.golang.org/grpc` to `1.85.0`.
* **Hardened documentation build lock**: `docs/requirements.in` floors `certifi` at `2026.10.19` (`2026.10.23` in `docs/requirements.txt`) for GHSA-6q48-6c7w-9gw2 and PYSEC-2026-4320.
* **Unchanged zero-dependency verifier**: `attestation`, `a2a`, `graph`, and `spec` check statements identically to `0.0.8`, released in lockstep ahead of `passmcp` `0.0.9`.
